更新内容：

### Miffan 自有改动

- 通知栏和流体云里的图标改成白色镂空样式，能看清米饭碗的眼睛、嘴巴和米饭；桌面图标选了蓝色大肥鱼时，通知里也会换成 Q 版大肥鱼。

### 同步自 RikkaHub

- 安装包大幅缩小：arm64 版从约 53 MB 降到约 27 MB，更新后还会清掉旧版本留下的约 13 MB 搜索词典。
- 修复快速连按两次返回键时应用闪退。
- 停止回复后马上发出的消息，不会再卡在队列里发不出去。
- 公式里带 `<`（如 0<x<1）时不再显示不全，带行距的换行（如 `\\[4pt]`）也不再被当成文字画出来。
- 拖动排序收藏的模型时不再来回抖动。
- 部分模型（如 qwen3-8b）不再被误判为能看图片。
- 新建或导入 AI 服务后，列表会自动滚到新加的那一项。
- 部分中转服务生成图片时，不再得到一张空白的灰图。
- 保存预览中的图片后，“正在保存”的提示会自动收起，不再和保存结果叠在一起。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- Notification and Fluid Cloud icons are now white with cut-out details, so the rice bowl's eyes, mouth and rice show; with the blue whale girl launcher icon, notifications use a chibi whale girl too.

### Synced from RikkaHub

- The app is much smaller: the arm64 build drops from about 53 MB to about 27 MB, and updating also clears an old search dictionary of about 13 MB.
- Fixed a crash when pressing Back twice very quickly.
- A message sent right after stopping a reply no longer gets stuck in the queue.
- Formulas containing `<` (such as 0<x<1) no longer show cut off, and line breaks with spacing (such as `\\[4pt]`) are no longer drawn as text.
- Reordering favorite models by dragging no longer jitters.
- Some models (such as qwen3-8b) are no longer mistaken for ones that can see images.
- After adding or importing an AI service, the list scrolls to the new entry.
- Some relay services no longer return a blank gray image when generating pictures.
- After saving an image from the preview, the "Saving" notice goes away instead of stacking with the result.
