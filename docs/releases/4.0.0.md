更新内容：

### Miffan 自有改动

- 新增“轻松聊天”模式：像用聊天软件一样和 AI 伙伴聊天，分为消息、伙伴、发现、我四个页面。新安装默认使用；升级用户首次打开时自行选择，之后可在“我 → 高级”或设置中随时切换，两种模式共用同一份数据。
- 每个伙伴一条连续的聊天记录：话题自动整理，一口气问几件事也会分别回答，并标出各自对应的问题；可以回复某条消息，或“只看这个话题”。隔一段时间再回来，伙伴会先和你打招呼。
- 伙伴能从聊天中学到你的偏好并调整自己的设定，每次调整都会提示并可撤销；“设定历史”可查看改动差异，并恢复到任意版本。
- “AI 对我的了解”集中展示伙伴记住的事和来源，可随时删除；伙伴现在会在后台主动整理记忆，不再依赖模型自己想起来记。
- 伙伴页提供推荐伙伴，可添加、删除伙伴并设置聊天背景；首次引导只需连接一次 AI 服务，还可以选蓝色大肥鱼作为第一个伙伴。
- 远程工作空间新增“远程屏幕”：在手机上查看并操作 macOS 或 Linux（wlroots 系 Wayland、X11）电脑的桌面，支持触控板模式和帧率调节，在按流量计费的网络下自动省流。GNOME 与 KDE 暂不支持。
- 伙伴可以操作你的电脑：可选“先问我”“直接操作”或“不允许”；伙伴操作时会显示提示，触摸画面即可接管，回复后保留操作步骤和最后一张截图。等待批准时直接发消息，会被当作对这次请求的回复，操作不会执行。
- 轻松聊天新增“我的电脑”和“连接电脑”引导：自动检测电脑环境，经你确认后安装或升级所需组件，最后让伙伴试看一眼屏幕。
- 新增批准请求通知，回复通知默认开启；每次授权如何处理都有记录可查；有新版本时在“我”页提示。
- 专业模式改进：输入框随内容自动增高，取代全屏编辑器；聊天中可直接显示助手在工作空间里留下的图片；通过自定义网关添加的模型也能自动识别工具调用和推理能力。同时移除了上游项目的 RikkaHub 搜索服务、赞助商内置服务商和捐赠页（已填写 API Key 的服务商会保留）。

### 同步自 RikkaHub

- 开启工作区后，联网搜索的结果和引用不再显示为空或被截断。
- 删除模型或编辑消息后，AI 回复仍会显示由哪个模型生成。
- 设置改为在后台读取，修改设置时不再刷新整个界面，启动后也不再整页重绘。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- New "Easy chat" mode: chat with AI partners the way you use a messaging app, with Chats, Partners, Discover and Me tabs. It is the default for new installs; upgrading users choose on first launch and can switch any time under "Me → Advanced" or in Settings. Both modes share the same data.
- One continuous conversation per partner: topics are organized automatically, several questions sent at once each get their own answer that shows which question it replies to, and you can reply to a message or view "Only this topic". Come back after a while and your partner greets you first.
- Partners learn your preferences from the conversation and adjust their own settings, with a notice and Undo each time; "Settings history" shows what changed and can restore any version.
- "What AI knows about me" collects what your partners remember and where it came from, and you can delete any of it. Partners now curate memories in the background instead of relying on the model to remember to save them.
- The Partners tab offers recommended partners, lets you add or delete partners and set a chat background; onboarding only asks you to connect an AI service once, and you can pick Blue Whale as your first partner.
- Remote workspaces gain "Remote screen": view and operate a macOS or Linux (wlroots Wayland, X11) desktop from your phone, with a trackpad mode, frame rate control and automatic data saving on metered networks. GNOME and KDE are not supported yet.
- Partners can operate your computer: choose "Ask first", "Automatic" or "Off". A banner shows while the partner is working, touching the screen takes over, and the reply keeps the steps and the last screenshot. Sending a message while an approval is pending answers that request, and the action does not run.
- Easy chat adds "My computers" and a "Connect a computer" guide that checks the computer, installs or upgrades what it needs after you confirm, and lets your partner take a first look at the screen.
- Notifications for approval requests, with reply notifications on by default; every permission decision is recorded for later review; the Me tab shows when a new version is available.
- Professional mode: the input grows with its content instead of opening a fullscreen editor; images the assistant leaves in a workspace show directly in chat; models added through custom gateways now get their tool-calling and reasoning abilities recognized. The upstream project's RikkaHub search service, sponsor built-in providers and donation page are removed (providers with an API key you entered are kept).

### Synced from RikkaHub

- Web search results and citations no longer show up empty or truncated when a workspace is enabled.
- AI replies still show which model generated them after the model is deleted or the message is edited.
- Settings are read in the background, so changing a setting no longer refreshes the whole interface and the app no longer redraws every page after launch.
