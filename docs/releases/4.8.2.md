更新内容：

### Miffan 自有改动

- 新版本提醒更及时：Miffan 会在打开时和每天一次检查新版本，不用重启也能发现；在「关于 Miffan」里也能随时手动检查。
- 全新的更新页面：按条列出这次改了什么，下载进度直接显示在按钮上，还可以选「稍后提醒」或「跳过这个版本」。
- 新增「更新设置」：可以选只标红点、红点加系统通知，或者先别提醒；还能开启 Wi-Fi 下自动下载新版本（默认关闭）。
- 下载中断能接着下：网络断了，已经下好的部分会保留；下好的安装包重新打开 Miffan 后也能直接安装。
- 第一次安装更新更顺：先说明要打开哪个系统开关，打开后回来会自动继续安装。
- 装好后讲讲新变化：更新完成后，聊天页顶部会出现一次「这次多了什么」，点开就能看。

### 同步自 RikkaHub

- 本次无面向用户的改动。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- Timelier update reminders: Miffan checks for new versions when you open it and once a day, so you no longer need a restart to notice one. You can also check any time in About Miffan.
- A new update page: it lists what changed item by item, shows download progress right on the button, and lets you choose Remind me later or Skip this version.
- New Update settings: choose a red dot only, a red dot plus a notification, or no reminders for now. You can also turn on downloading new versions over Wi-Fi (off by default).
- Downloads resume: if the network drops, what was already downloaded is kept, and a finished download can still be installed after you reopen Miffan.
- A smoother first install: Miffan first explains which system switch to turn on, then continues installing when you come back.
- What's new after updating: once an update is installed, a card at the top of Chats shows what changed this time.

### Synced from RikkaHub

- No user-facing changes in this release.
