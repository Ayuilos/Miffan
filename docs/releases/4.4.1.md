### Miffan 自有改动

- 几个伙伴共用一台电脑时，同一时间只有一个伙伴在操作：一个伙伴开始操作后，这一轮回复结束前别的伙伴不会插手，会被告知是谁在用、先做不需要电脑的部分；群聊里有几位成员共用同一台电脑时，也会提醒它们分工。
- 屏幕页顶部的横幅会写出是哪个伙伴正在操作这台电脑，伙伴的屏幕页和「我的电脑」里的屏幕页显示一致。
- 电脑页的定时任务列表会显示每个任务是哪个伙伴安排的：左边是它的头像，右下角标出任务类型，说明末尾带上它的名字。
- 专业模式下，助手欢迎页里的最近对话只显示这个助手自己的对话，不再混入其他助手的对话。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- When several partners share one computer, only one operates it at a time: once a partner starts, the others stay out until its reply ends, and are told who is using it and to do the parts that need no computer first; group chats with several members on the same computer also remind them to split the work.
- The banner at the top of the screen page names the partner operating the computer, the same on a partner's screen page and on the screen page under My Computers.
- The scheduled task list on a computer's page shows which partner set up each task: its avatar on the left with the task type in the corner, and its name at the end of the description.
- In professional mode, the recent chats on an assistant's welcome page only show that assistant's own chats instead of chats from other assistants.
