更新内容：

### Miffan 自有改动

- 轻松聊天中，伙伴连续执行多步操作，或改动了文件、命令、记忆等内容时，回复里会显示操作记录：执行中显示当前步骤和步数，完成后可展开查看每一步，失败的步骤会标出。
- 修复伙伴直接执行指令、还没说话时，回复气泡显示为空的问题。
- “伙伴资料”新增“模型”：可以查看并更换伙伴使用的模型；未单独设置时显示所用默认模型的名称。
- 修复对话有时一直停在“正在思考…”、应用变卡甚至无响应，以及停止后刚发送的消息丢失的问题。
- 伙伴操作电脑时，连接会话过期后会自动恢复，不再导致后续操作全部失败；在桌面上指定位置输入文字时，会先点击该位置。
- “关于”页新增“使用文档”入口，直达官网的使用文档。
- 远程屏幕在触控板和直接点按模式之间切换时，画面不再闪动。

### 同步自 RikkaHub

- 本次无面向用户的改动。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- In easy chat, when a partner takes several steps in a row or changes something such as files, commands or memory, the reply shows a step record: the current step and count while it works, and afterwards a list you can expand, with failed steps marked.
- Fixed an empty reply bubble when a partner starts acting on a request before saying anything.
- **Partner profile** now has **Model**: see and change the model a partner uses; without its own choice it shows the name of the default model it uses.
- Fixed chats sometimes staying on "Thinking…", the app slowing down or not responding, and the message just sent being lost after stopping.
- When a partner operates a computer, an expired connection session now recovers on its own instead of failing every later step; typing at a position on the desktop clicks there first.
- The **About** page has a new **User guide** entry that opens the online user guide.
- The remote screen no longer flickers when switching between touchpad and direct touch.

### Synced from RikkaHub

- No user-facing changes in this release.
