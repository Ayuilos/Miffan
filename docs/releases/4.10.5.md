### Miffan 自有改动

- 伙伴协作实时显示执行步骤：在伙伴私聊或群聊中，伙伴执行多步任务时不再一直停留在「正在连接…」，而是实时显示当前步骤和思考状态，直到回复完成。
- 伙伴互聊支持提问与交互终端确认：当受托伙伴中途向你提问，或需要你打开终端输入密码等交互操作时，现在可以在伙伴聊天里直接回答或打开终端处理，完成后伙伴会接着执行。
- 随时直达伙伴聊天与待确认提醒：伙伴联系另一位伙伴期间，对话里的状态行会直接提供「查看」入口；如果对方中途需要你确认，对话顶部和状态行都会高亮提示「需要你确认」，消息列表也会优先显示「等你处理」，点一下即可前往处理。

### Miffan changes

- Live steps during partner chats: In private chats between partners and group chats, a partner running multi-step work now shows its live steps and thinking status instead of staying on "Connecting…" until it finishes.
- Answer questions and open terminals in partner chats: When a partner working on another partner's request asks you a question or needs an interactive terminal (such as entering a password), you can now answer or open the terminal directly inside the partner chat so it can continue.
- Faster jump to partner chats and approval alerts: While a partner is contacting another partner, the status line in your conversation now offers a "View" link right away; if the other partner needs your OK mid-task, both a top banner and the status line highlight it, and the Chats list prioritizes "Waiting for you" so you can jump straight there.
