### Miffan 自有改动

- 设置可以交给伙伴：在聊天里说一句“帮我连上 DeepSeek”或“字大一点”，伙伴就能帮你连接 AI 服务、添加模型、换伙伴用的模型，也能调主题、深浅色、字号、通知和对你的称呼。每次改动先列在卡片上，你确认后才生效，随时能撤销。第一次用时，伙伴会请你打开「调整设置」能力。
- Key 只填进安全卡片：需要 API Key 时，伙伴会发一张安全卡片，Key 直接存进手机，伙伴自始至终看不到。搜索服务的 Key、MCP 和模型的鉴权请求头也是这样，而且都加密保存。
- Key 误打进聊天框会被拦下：消息里有像 Key 的内容时，会先问你要不要发。如果聊天里正好有一张等着填的安全卡片，点一下就能把 Key 挪进卡片。
- 伙伴能调伙伴：想多久再回、回复多长、带多少聊天记录、记忆开关，伙伴能调自己的，也能调别的伙伴的。
- 朗读和语音输入：伙伴能帮你连接朗读和语音输入服务，朗读服务连上后会先读一句给你听；还能选音色、语速和音频格式。通过 OpenRouter 用 Fish Audio 这类模型时，也能填模型和音色。
- 回复自动朗读：轻松模式下伙伴回复后可以自动读出来，在「我 → 朗读」里开关。
- 备份提醒：轻松模式会在聊天页顶部提醒你备份，间隔可以自己选，也可以让伙伴帮你设。
- 伙伴知道发生了什么：你撤销了它的改动，或者改了它的设定和权限，它下一次回复时就会知道。它也能自己翻看授权记录和资料改动记录。
- OpenRouter 朗读恢复正常：原来用的朗读模型已经下线，现在换成了新的默认模型，旧设置会自动迁移。
- 其他修复：
  - 轻松模式的字号设置现在对聊天气泡也生效。
  - 修复了轻松模式聊天偶尔滑不动、要退出重进才恢复的问题。
  - 回复中途断开时，已经写出的结尾不会再丢。
  - 换回能调用工具的模型后，“模型不能调用工具”的提示会消失，不再一直留在底部。
  - 装了 KDE Plasma 6.8 的电脑切到普通远程桌面模式时能正常连接了。

### Miffan changes

- Leave settings to your partners: say "connect DeepSeek for me" or "make the text bigger" in a chat, and a partner can connect AI services, add models and switch the model a partner uses, or change the theme, light or dark, text size, notifications and what partners call you. Every change is listed on a card first, takes effect only after you confirm, and can be undone at any time. The first time, the partner asks you to turn on its "Adjust settings" ability.
- Keys go only into a secure card: when an API key is needed, the partner sends a secure card; the key is saved straight to the phone and the partner never sees it. Search service keys and authorization headers for MCP and models work the same way, and all of them are stored encrypted.
- Keys typed into the chat box are caught: when a message holds something that looks like a key, you are asked before it is sent. If a secure card is waiting in the chat, one tap moves the key into it.
- Partners can adjust partners: how long a partner thinks, how long its replies get, how much of the chat it is sent, and whether memory is on; a partner can change its own and other partners'.
- Read-aloud and voice input: partners can connect read-aloud and voice input services and read you a sample once read-aloud is connected; they can also pick the voice, speed and audio format. With models such as Fish Audio through OpenRouter, they can set the model and voice too.
- Replies read aloud automatically: in the easy interface, replies can be read aloud as they arrive; turn it on or off under Me → Read aloud.
- Backup reminder: the easy interface reminds you to back up at the top of Chats; pick how often, or ask a partner to set it.
- Partners know what happened: when you undo a partner's change or edit its profile or permissions, it knows on its next reply. It can also look up its approval and profile change records itself.
- OpenRouter read-aloud works again: the model it used was retired; it now uses a new default model, and saved settings move over automatically.
- Other fixes:
  - Text size now applies to chat bubbles in the easy interface.
  - Fixed easy chats sometimes refusing to scroll until you left and came back.
  - A reply that breaks off keeps the end it had already written.
  - After switching back to a model that can call tools, the "model cannot call tools" notice goes away instead of staying at the bottom.
  - Computers running KDE Plasma 6.8 connect again in the normal remote desktop mode.
