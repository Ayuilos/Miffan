### Miffan 自有改动

- 自建中转支持高性能模式：在自建 VPS 中转开启 UDP 转发并更新电脑端连接程序后，不在家也能通过 Sunshine 高性能模式看电脑画面并听声音（包括 Windows 电脑），码率按中转上限自动适配；使用 Miffan 免费中转或未开启 UDP 转发时，仍自动使用截图或普通连接。
- 中转检测显示高性能支持状态：电脑详情里的「检测连接」现在会实际探测高性能模式是否可用，区分「支持」「需更新电脑端」「UDP 不可达」和「在外不支持」，方便排查自建中转的网络配置。
- 连接程序下载更稳且防误降级：安装或更新电脑端连接程序时优先从国内更快的镜像下载，失败或校验不符会自动回退到 GitHub；在家里连上电脑时也不会把已安装的更新版连接程序误降级为旧版。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- High-performance mode over self-hosted relays: When your self-hosted VPS relay enables UDP forwarding and the computer's connector is updated, you can use Sunshine high-performance streaming with audio away from home (including on Windows), with bitrate capped to the relay limit; Miffan's free relay and relays without UDP forwarding continue to fall back to snapshots or normal connections.
- Relay diagnostics show high-performance availability: "Test connection" in computer details now probes whether high-performance streaming works over the relay, distinguishing between Supported, Update PC, UDP blocked, and Not away.
- More reliable connector downloads without accidental downgrades: Installing or updating the computer connector now tries a faster mirror first and falls back to GitHub automatically on failure or checksum mismatch, and connecting at home no longer downgrades a newer connector already installed on the computer.
