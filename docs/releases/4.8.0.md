### Miffan 自有改动

- 出门在外也能用电脑：不在电脑所在的网络时，伙伴可以经 Miffan 免费中转连回电脑，不用装 Tailscale，也不用改路由器。连接全程加密，电脑只认你的手机。免费中转每台电脑每天可用 60 分钟。
- 连接电脑时新增一步「不在家的时候，也想用这台电脑吗？」，点「开启」大约半分钟就能设好。已经连上的电脑，可以在详情页的「出门在外」里打开。
- Miffan 会自动在家里的地址和中转之间选更快的那条；回到同一个网络后，自动改回直接连接。
- 经中转也能看 Mac 和 Linux 的屏幕（省流画质）。Windows 在外时伙伴照常操作，画面暂时只能在同一个网络里看。
- 电脑详情新增「出门在外」：
  - 今天经中转用了多少分钟；
  - 「检测连接」，逐项查看线路和延迟；
  - 换一个中转；
  - 让电脑忘记这台手机。
- 可以用自己搭的中转：填地址和访问码，或者扫码添加；每部手机可以各用各的中转。中转是开源的，可以部署到 Cloudflare 或自己的服务器，使用文档里有教程。
- 打开伙伴的对话或电脑页时，会提前准备好电脑连接；伙伴连续在电脑上执行命令也更快了。
- 修复连接新电脑时，自动取的名字和已有电脑重名导致保存失败的问题。

### Miffan changes

- Use your computer away from home: when you're not on its network, partners reach it through Miffan's free relay, with no Tailscale and no router changes. The connection is encrypted end to end and the computer only accepts your phone. The free relay gives each computer 60 minutes a day.
- Connecting a computer now asks "Use this computer away from home too?"; tap Turn on and it's set up in about half a minute. For computers you already have, turn it on under Away from home on the computer's page.
- Miffan picks the faster of the home address and the relay by itself, and goes back to a direct connection once you're on the same network again.
- The screens of Mac and Linux computers can be viewed through the relay too, in data-saving quality. Away from home a Windows computer can still be used by partners, but its screen can only be shown on the same network for now.
- A new Away from home section on the computer's page:
  - today's relay minutes;
  - Test the connection, which checks the route and latency step by step;
  - switching to another relay;
  - making the computer forget this phone.
- Use a relay you host yourself: enter its address and access code, or scan its QR code; each phone can use its own relay. The relay is open source and runs on Cloudflare or your own server; the guide shows how.
- A partner's computer connection is prepared while its chat or computer page is open, and running several commands in a row is faster.
- Fixed saving a new computer failing when the name Miffan picked for it was already taken.
