更新内容：

### Miffan 自有改动

- 打开更快、用起来更顺：启动时把用不上的准备工作挪到后台；数据库加了索引，打开长聊天、读取收藏、查找记录都更快；消息列表和伙伴页只在聊天真的有变化时才重新整理，群聊不再每来一条消息就重读全部记录。
- 长聊天滑得更顺：滑回看过的消息不用再解析一遍格式；聊天里的电脑截图按卡片大小解码；伙伴回复时，每收到一段文字只更新正在写的那条，不再把屏幕上所有消息都重新算一遍。
- 长回复看得到最新内容：伙伴写长回复时，画面会跟着最新的文字往下走，不再停在开头、写完才突然跳到最后。
- 标签和搜索更顺手：从别的标签回来，列表还停在原来的地方，搜索词和折叠的分组也都还在；再点一下当前标签回到顶部。搜索页一打开就显示最近聊过的伙伴。
- 回复读起来更像消息：伙伴回复里的标题小一号，更接近正文；引用条只显示文字，不再带 `#`、`**` 这类符号。
- 输入改成底部面板：改名字、起分组名、挑伙伴都从底部升起的面板里完成，输入框贴着键盘，点「好了」就完成。
- 出错时说人话：翻译、画图、备份恢复、定时任务失败时，告诉你发生了什么、可以怎么做，不再弹出一串技术报错；画图碰到服务的内容限制时会明确说出来。
- 几处页面细节：「伙伴记得的我」换成卡片，删除改成图标按钮，还没有记忆时会说明伙伴会记下什么；伙伴资料页往下滑时，顶栏显示头像和名字；日期显示为「10月31日」这样的写法，「我」页的语言显示为「简体中文」。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- Faster to open, smoother to use: work the first screen doesn't need now runs in the background at launch; new database indexes make opening long chats, loading favorites and looking up records faster; the Chats list and Partners tab only rework a chat when it actually changed, and group chats no longer reread their whole history for every new message.
- Long chats scroll more smoothly: messages you scroll back to are not formatted again; computer screenshots in a chat are decoded at the card's size; while a partner replies, each new piece of text only updates the reply being written, not every message on screen.
- Long replies stay in view: while a partner writes a long reply, the chat follows the newest text instead of staying at its start and jumping to the end when it finishes.
- Tabs and search keep up with you: coming back from another tab, the list is where you left it, with its search and folded groups; tap the tab you are on to go back to the top. Search opens with the partners you talked to recently.
- Replies read like messages: headings in partner replies are closer to body size, and quote bars show plain text without marks like `#` or `**`.
- Typing in a sheet: renaming yourself or a group and picking a partner happen in a sheet rising from the bottom, with the field right above the keyboard and a Done button.
- Errors in plain words: when translating, drawing, restoring a backup or a scheduled task fails, Miffan says what happened and what to try instead of showing a technical error; drawing also says when a request ran into the service's content rules.
- Small page touches: What partners remember is shown as cards with an icon to delete, and explains what partners will note there while it is empty; a partner's profile shows their avatar and name in the top bar once you scroll down; dates use your language's own month-day form, and Me shows the language by its own name.

### Synced from RikkaHub

- Faster replies in chats with pictures: compressed pictures are cached, so later turns don't compress them again.
- Fixes and small improvements: fixed a possible crash when favoriting the same model twice quickly; deleting a conversation also deletes its favorited messages; the model capability catalog refreshes every 4 hours; lorebook entries in professional mode can be reordered by long-press dragging.
