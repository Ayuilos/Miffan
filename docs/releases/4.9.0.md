更新内容：

### Miffan 自有改动

- 更流畅：消息列表和伙伴页的状态灯只在聊天真的有变化时才重新整理，不再因为无关的设置改动整页重算；伙伴之间的群聊不再每来一条消息就重读全部记录。
- 长聊天滑得更顺：滑回看过的消息不用再解析一遍格式；聊天里的电脑截图按卡片大小解码，不再整张原图占内存；伙伴回复时，界面每收到一段文字只更新正在写的那条，不再把屏幕上所有消息都重新算一遍。
- 打开更快：启动时把用不上的准备工作挪到后台；数据库加了索引，打开长聊天、读取收藏、查找记录都更快；全文搜索的索引只更新改动过的消息。
- 长回复看得到最新内容：伙伴写长回复时，画面会跟着最新的文字往下走，不再停在开头、写完才突然跳到最后。
- 切换底部标签不丢位置：从别的标签回来，列表还停在原来的地方，搜索词和折叠的分组也都还在。再点一下当前标签，列表回到顶部。
- 搜索页一打开就有内容：显示最近聊过的伙伴，点一下直接进聊天；下面提示能搜什么。
- 引用条只显示文字：回复里引用的原话不再带 `#`、`**` 这类格式符号。
- 出错时说人话：翻译、画图、备份恢复、定时任务失败时，告诉你发生了什么、可以怎么做，不再弹出一串技术报错。画图碰到服务的内容限制时会明确说出来。
- 聊天里的标题小一号：伙伴回复里的大标题更接近正文大小，读起来像消息而不是文档。
- 改名字、起分组名、挑伙伴这类操作改成从底部升起的面板，输入框贴着键盘，点「好了」就完成。
- 「伙伴记得的我」换成卡片样式，删除改成图标按钮；还没有记忆时，会说明伙伴会记下什么。
- 伙伴资料页往下滑时，顶栏会显示伙伴的头像和名字。
- 日期写法更自然：消息列表、收藏和搜索结果里显示为「10月31日」；「我」页的语言显示为「简体中文」。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- Smoother: the Chats list and the Partners tab's status lights only rework a chat when it actually changed, not on every unrelated settings change; group chats between partners no longer reread their whole history for every new message.
- Long chats scroll more smoothly: messages you scroll back to are not formatted again; computer screenshots in a chat are decoded at the card's size instead of full resolution; while a partner replies, each new piece of text only updates the reply being written, not every message on screen.
- Faster to open: work the first screen doesn't need now runs in the background at launch; new database indexes make opening long chats, loading favorites and looking up records faster; the full-text search index only updates the messages that changed.
- Long replies stay in view: while a partner writes a long reply, the chat follows the newest text instead of staying at its start and jumping to the end when it finishes.
- Tabs keep their place: coming back from another tab, the list is where you left it, with its search and folded groups. Tap the tab you are on to go back to the top.
- Search opens with something to show: partners you talked to recently, one tap away, and a line saying what you can search for.
- Quote bars show plain text: quoted messages no longer show formatting marks like `#` or `**`.
- Errors in plain words: when translating, drawing, restoring a backup or a scheduled task fails, Miffan says what happened and what to try instead of showing a technical error. Drawing also says when a request ran into the service's content rules.
- Smaller headings in chats: headings in partner replies are closer to body size, so a reply reads like a message, not a document.
- Renaming yourself or a group and picking a partner now happen in a sheet rising from the bottom, with the field right above the keyboard and a Done button.
- What partners remember is shown as cards with an icon to delete; with nothing remembered yet, the page explains what partners will note there.
- On a partner's profile, the top bar shows their avatar and name once you scroll down.
- More natural dates: lists, favorites and search results show dates in your language's own month-day form, and Me shows the language by its own name.

### Synced from RikkaHub

- Faster replies in chats with pictures: compressed pictures are cached, so later turns don't compress them again.
- Fixed a possible crash when favoriting the same model twice quickly.
- Deleting a conversation also deletes its favorited messages (on the professional-mode history page, once the undo window has passed).
- The model capability catalog refreshes every 4 hours, so new models get their capability details sooner.
- Lorebook entries in professional mode can be reordered by long-press dragging.
