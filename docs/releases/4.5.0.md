### Miffan 自有改动

- 伙伴页改版：伙伴像通讯录一样一行一个，显示一句话简介、是否在忙、能否用电脑；可以搜索，分组能折叠并标出人数；底部的推荐模板可以直接添加，也能自己捏一个。
- 伙伴资料页重新整理：顶部是发消息、看屏幕、编辑资料三个快捷按钮，能力、记忆、模型等设置分组排列；名字和性格在单独的编辑页里改，没保存就离开会先提醒；伙伴用电脑的设置有了自己的页面。
- 新的“换个样子”页：大预览下可以挑角色（四种米饭、蓝色大肥鱼、照片或表情）、配色（也可以跟着 App 换颜色）和动作性格；在资料页点头像就能直接进入，新建伙伴时还能随机换一个。表情头像会跟着头像大小放大。
- “我”页改成分组卡片，外观、通知、备份都有了更简洁的页面：外观页能实时预览深浅色、主题色、角色主题和桌面图标，备份页会提醒你多久没备份了。
- “发现”页改版：画图和翻译两个大入口，下面是收藏的消息和最近的画作。新的画图页用“服务连接”里选的画图模型，可以加参考图、选张数；收藏页可以搜索、按伙伴筛选、按月份分组；翻译页在输入框下方选目标语言，从底部抽屉里挑，结果可以复制或朗读。
- 聊天里长按伙伴的消息就能收藏。
- “服务连接”页改成分组卡片：已连接的服务商各占一行，默认模型、后台整理、画图、翻译分别用哪个模型一目了然，翻译模型也能在这里改；翻译没有单独选模型时，直接用默认聊天模型。
- 选模型的列表（默认模型、后台整理、伙伴模型、翻译）和自动选模型时，不再出现只会出图的模型。
- 新增内置供应商 Magpie：在电脑上打开 Magpie 的局域网共享，在手机上填入地址和密钥，就能用电脑上的各家模型。
- “我的电脑”里的“Agent 工具”改叫“电脑上的 AI 工具”，说法更好懂；新增文案都补齐了日、韩、俄语翻译。

### Miffan changes

- Redesigned Partners tab: partners are listed one per row like contacts, with a one-line intro and whether they are busy or can use a computer; search, collapsible groups with member counts, and suggested templates at the bottom to add directly or build your own.
- Reorganized partner profiles: quick buttons for Message, View screen and Edit at the top, with abilities, memory and model settings grouped below; name and personality are edited on their own page, which asks before leaving unsaved changes; a partner's computer settings have their own page.
- A new "Change look" page: with a large preview, pick the character (four Miffans, the blue whale, a photo or an emoji), its colors (or match the app's colors) and how it moves; tap the avatar on a profile to open it, or let a new partner surprise you with a random look. Emoji avatars now scale with the avatar size.
- The Me tab is now grouped cards, with simpler Appearance, Notifications and Backup pages: Appearance previews light/dark mode, theme color, the character theme and the launcher icon live, and Backup reminds you how long it has been since your last backup.
- Redesigned Discover tab: two large entries for Draw and Translate, followed by saved messages and recent drawings. The new Draw page uses the drawing model chosen under AI service, with reference images and a picture count; Saved messages can be searched, filtered by partner and grouped by month; Translate picks the target language below the input from a bottom sheet, and results can be copied or read aloud.
- Long-press a partner's message in a chat to save it.
- The AI service page is now grouped cards: each connected service on its own row, and which model handles chat, background tasks, drawing and translation at a glance, including changing the translation model there; without a translation model of its own, translation uses the default chat model.
- Model lists (default, background, partner and translation models) and automatic model choices no longer offer models that only make images.
- New built-in provider Magpie: turn on Magpie's local network sharing on your computer, enter its address and key on your phone, and use the models available on that computer.
- "Agent tools" under My Computers is now called "AI tools on your computers"; all new text is translated into Japanese, Korean and Russian.
