### Miffan 自有改动

- 伙伴请求权限时改用更易读的卡片：用一句话说明要做什么，列出关键信息，需要时可展开查看原始调用；轻松模式和专业模式都使用这种卡片。
- 一条回复里有多个普通请求时，可以「全部允许」；调用 MCP 工具、添加日程、写入文件这几类操作可以设为「以后这类都直接做」，随时能在授权记录里收回。
- 添加日程前可以先调整时间，添加后会以日程卡片显示。
- 点开已处理的记录可以看到具体改了什么，并能撤销和重做；撤销只还原这一次的改动，不会影响之后无关的修改。
- 伙伴向你提问时会显示问题卡片，可以点选项或自己写回答，暂时离开聊天也不会丢掉填了一半的答案。
- 修复伙伴修改扩展设置成功后，记录却显示失败、只能看到原始数据的问题。
- 手机上没有可写入的日历时（如部分 ColorOS 机型），Miffan 会自动创建一个本地日历来存放日程。
- 消息页的搜索改为在当前页面展开，不再跳转到新页面。
- 聊天更连贯：开启新话题后，伙伴仍能看到最近的聊天原文和更详细的旧话题摘要，隔一段时间再聊也不容易被误分成新话题。
- 自动开启新话题的地方会显示一条分隔线，点「接着上个话题聊」可以接回原话题，接回后也能撤销；「+」菜单里可以主动换个话题；隔一段时间回来时，点「查看历史对话」后发的消息会优先接着上次的话题聊。

### Miffan changes

- Permission requests from a partner now appear as readable cards: one sentence saying what it wants to do, the key details listed, and the raw call available on demand. Easy and professional modes both use these cards.
- When one reply makes several ordinary requests, you can "Allow all". MCP tools, adding calendar events and writing files can be set to "always allow", and you can take that back any time in the permission history.
- You can adjust a calendar event's time before it is added, and it shows as an event card afterwards.
- Opening a settled record shows exactly what changed, with undo and redo. Undo reverts only that change and leaves later unrelated edits alone.
- When a partner asks you a question, a question card lets you tap an option or write your own answer, and a half-finished answer survives leaving the chat.
- Fixed a successful extension settings change showing as failed, with only raw data to look at.
- When the phone has no writable calendar (as on some ColorOS phones), Miffan creates a local calendar for your events.
- Search on the Chats tab now opens in place instead of on a new page.
- Chats stay more continuous: after a new topic starts, the partner still sees recent messages and a fuller summary of earlier topics, and picking up again after a while is less likely to split off a new topic.
- A divider marks where a new topic started on its own. Tap "Continue the last topic" to put it back, and undo that if you change your mind. You can change the topic yourself from the "+" menu, and after a while away, messages sent after tapping "View earlier chats" pick up the last topic first.
