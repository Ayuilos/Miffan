### Miffan 自有改动

- 经中转连得更快：直连被挡住时不再干等，挑选中转入口不再拖慢第一次连接，连上电脑少走好几个来回，打开屏幕时几路连接同时建立。
- 换网络后恢复更快：手机切换网络时会马上检查正在用的中转连接，断了立刻重连。
- 慢网络下更稳：上传慢的时候，保持连接的信号不再被挤到后面，连接不容易被误判断开。
- 更省中转额度：截图画面 30 秒没变化就暂停刷新，显示「画面静止 · 轻点刷新」，伙伴一操作就自动恢复；屏幕页退到后台后不再占着中转；和伙伴聊天时不再提前经中转连接电脑。
- 屏幕断开会说原因：画面太久没动、发得太快被限速，或者手机换了网络，断开时都会说明原因和该怎么做。
- 电脑上的中转程序会自动更新：在家直连电脑时，Miffan 会在后台把电脑上的中转程序更新到新版，数据合并发送更省额度，打开连接更快，也不会再因为别人反复连错而把你的手机暂时挡在门外。
- 闲置的小电脑能找到了：伙伴被删除或换了新的小电脑后，留下的那台会在电脑页显示为「闲置的小电脑」，可以分给别的伙伴，也可以删除腾出内存。
- 小电脑不再被悄悄留下：给已经有小电脑的伙伴再建一台时，可以选新的建好后删掉旧的；删除伙伴时也可以一起删掉它的小电脑。

### Miffan changes

- Faster connections through the relay: Miffan no longer waits on a blocked direct connection, picking a relay entry no longer slows the first connection, connecting to a computer takes fewer round trips, and a screen opens its connections side by side.
- Quicker recovery after a network change: when your phone switches networks, Miffan checks the relay connections in use right away and reconnects any that dropped.
- Steadier on slow networks: on a slow upload, the signals that keep a connection alive no longer get stuck behind other data, so connections are less often mistaken for dropped.
- Uses less relay quota: a snapshot screen pauses after 30 seconds without change and shows "Still · Tap to refresh", resuming as soon as the partner acts; a screen page in the background no longer holds the relay; partner chats no longer connect to the computer through the relay in advance.
- Screens say why they closed: when the relay ends a screen because nothing moved for a while, data came too fast, or your phone changed networks, Miffan tells you why and what to do.
- The relay program on your computer updates itself: when you connect to a computer directly at home, Miffan updates its relay program in the background. The new version batches data to use less quota, opens connections faster, and no longer lets someone else's repeated failed attempts briefly lock your phone out.
- Idle mini boxes are no longer lost: when a partner is deleted or gets a new mini box, the one left behind shows on the computer's page as an idle mini box, which you can give to another partner or delete to free memory.
- No more forgotten mini boxes: when setting up a new mini box for a partner that already has one, you can delete the old one once the new one is ready; deleting a partner can delete its mini box too.
