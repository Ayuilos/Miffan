### Miffan 自有改动

- 新增高性能模式：由电脑上的 Sunshine 用显卡编码画面，延迟更低、画面更流畅，支持 Linux 和 Mac。画面直连传输并始终加密，配对和管理仍经 SSH；条件不满足时自动改用普通连接，并说明原因。
- GNOME 和 KDE 桌面改用系统自带的远程桌面（RDP），由 Miffan 自动启动并生成登录密码，支持中文输入和剪贴板；电脑证书变化时会先请你确认。
- 屏幕页新增“省流量 / 均衡 / 最清晰”三档画面，取代原来的帧率选项；使用移动数据时默认省流量，并提示大约的流量消耗。
- 新增全屏横屏模式；高性能模式下可以开启电脑的声音。
- 顶部状态行显示当前的连接方式和已用流量，点一下可查看连接详情；菜单里可以临时改用普通连接，或改用高性能模式重连。
- 轻松模式的电脑设置和屏幕页都能一键启用高性能模式，依次完成启动 Sunshine、加密设置和配对；Mac 还可以直接在电脑上打开所需的权限设置页面。
- “你在操作，伙伴已暂停”只在伙伴确实受影响时出现，并会自动收起；全屏时改为显示在角落的小按钮中。
- 触控板模式下，高性能模式会先显示指针将到的位置，操作更跟手。
- 修复 Mac 上无法输入中文的问题；Mac 屏幕的传输速度明显提升。
- 修复“重新连接”有时失败的问题；当设置的固定屏幕端口无法连接时，会说明原因并可一键改用自动连接。

### Miffan changes

- New high-performance mode: Sunshine on your computer encodes the screen with its graphics card for lower latency and smoother motion, on Linux and Mac. The picture travels directly and is always encrypted, while pairing and management still go through SSH; when that is not possible, Miffan falls back to the normal connection and tells you why.
- GNOME and KDE desktops now use their built-in Remote Desktop (RDP). Miffan starts it and generates the sign-in password, with Chinese input and clipboard support, and asks you to confirm when the computer's certificate changes.
- The screen page has three picture levels (Data saver / Balanced / Sharpest) in place of the old frame-rate options. On mobile data it starts at Data saver and tells you roughly how much data it uses.
- New full-screen landscape mode; high-performance mode can also play the computer's sound.
- The status line shows how the screen is connected and how much data it has used; tap it for connection details. The menu can switch to the normal connection for this visit, or reconnect with high-performance mode.
- Easy mode can turn on high-performance mode in one step from computer setup or from the screen page: it starts Sunshine, sets up encryption and pairs. On a Mac it can also open the needed permission settings right on the computer.
- "You are operating, the partner is paused" appears only when the partner is actually affected and shrinks away on its own; in full screen it moves into the small corner buttons.
- In touchpad mode, high-performance mode shows where the pointer is heading before the picture catches up, so it feels more responsive.
- Fixed Chinese input on Mac; the Mac screen now transfers noticeably faster.
- Fixed "Reconnect" sometimes failing; when a fixed screen port cannot be reached, Miffan explains why and offers automatic connection in one tap.
