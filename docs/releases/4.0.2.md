### Miffan 自有改动

- 远程屏幕新增“操作说明”：第一次使用直接点按或触控板模式时自动弹出，之后可在更多菜单里随时查看两种模式下各个手势的作用。
- 双指操作更接近 Mac 触控板：双指滑动不再被误认成缩放，滑得越快滚得越远，松手后会继续惯性滚动；双指捏合缩放电脑里的内容，双指轻点为右键。右下角的 + 和 − 用来放大或缩小手机上的画面。
- 连接 Mac 时，右下角新增桌面按钮，可切换左右桌面、打开调度中心或显示当前应用的所有窗口。三指和四指手势容易被手机系统拦截，不再发送到电脑。
- 隔一段时间回到伙伴聊天并发消息后，伙伴形象不再在回复结束时飞回欢迎页；欢迎区域会收起，只保留上次聊天时间和“查看历史对话”。
- 修复轻松聊天的消息、伙伴、发现和“我”四个页面上滑时，内容透到状态栏后面的问题。
- 修复打开远程终端后立刻输入时，命令偶尔丢失或终端没有反应的问题。
- “我的电脑”相关页面补全繁体中文、日语、韩语和俄语翻译。
- 启动时把读取设置等工作移出主线程，减少首次启动卡顿，以及启动过程中触摸屏幕出现“应用无响应”的情况。

### Miffan changes

- Remote screen adds "How to use": it opens the first time you use direct touch or trackpad mode, and you can open it from the overflow menu any time to see what each gesture does in both modes.
- Two-finger input feels closer to a Mac trackpad: scrolling is no longer mistaken for zooming, faster swipes scroll further and keep gliding after you lift your fingers; pinching zooms the content on the computer, and a two-finger tap is a right-click. The + and − buttons in the corner zoom the picture on your phone.
- On a Mac, a new desktop button in the corner switches to the left or right desktop, opens Mission Control or shows all windows of the current app. Three- and four-finger gestures, which phone systems often intercept, are no longer sent to the computer.
- After coming back to a partner and sending a message, the partner no longer flies back to the welcome when the reply ends; the welcome shrinks to the last-chat time and "View earlier chats".
- Fixed content showing behind the status bar while scrolling Easy chat's Chats, Partners, Discover and Me tabs.
- Fixed commands typed right after opening a remote terminal sometimes being lost or the terminal not responding.
- The "My computers" screens are now translated into Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Russian.
- Startup work such as loading settings moved off the main thread, reducing first-launch stalls and "App isn't responding" when touching the screen during startup.
