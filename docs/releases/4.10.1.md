### Miffan 自有改动

- 提问方式更灵活：伙伴能按事情选用单选、多选、滑杆、步进器、日期时间、开关、排序或填写文字，也能把问题分页展示、根据前面的回答决定后续问题。
- 批准前可以改参数：在日程、写入文件、下载文件和外部工具的确认卡片上，你可以先改标题、时间、内容等参数再允许执行；涉及命令、凭据和权限的卡片仍保持锁定。
- 伙伴知道你改过：你修改参数后，伙伴会知道这是你的最新要求，不会误以为是自己弄错了，也不会擅自改回去。
- 完成记录更清楚：已经回答的问题会折叠成答案摘要，需要时可以展开；你改过参数的操作和日程会标上“已由你修改”。

### Miffan changes

- More flexible questions: Partners can use single or multiple choice, sliders, steppers, date and time pickers, switches, reordering, or text input, with paged questions and follow-ups based on earlier answers.
- Edit before approving: On calendar, file-writing, download, and external-tool approval cards, you can change parameters such as titles, times, and content before allowing the action. Command, credential, and permission cards remain locked.
- Partners recognize your edits: After you change parameters, the partner knows they reflect your latest intent and will not mistake them for its own error or revert them.
- Clearer finished records: Answered questions fold into an answer summary that can be expanded, and actions and calendar events you edited are marked as edited by you.
