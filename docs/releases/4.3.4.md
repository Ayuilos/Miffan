### Miffan 自有改动

- 回复里的链接可以正常打开了：紧跟在中文后面的网址也能识别，遇到中文标点会正确断开；www. 开头的网址可以直接打开，打不开时会给出提示；指向工作区文件的链接会在 App 内打开文件预览。
- 轻松模式下，回复里的步骤记录和电脑操作卡片按实际发生的顺序显示，不再固定把步骤放在顶部、电脑操作放在底部。
- 隔一段时间再打开伙伴时，上次聊天最后的记忆变动和回复会留在「上次聊天」分隔线上方，不会再被挤到这次的欢迎页下面。
- 远程工作区里，从文件预览跳到所在文件夹时能打开正确的位置，不再因为路径重复而找不到文件夹。

Updates:

### Miffan changes

- Links in replies open properly: URLs right after Chinese text are recognized and end at Chinese punctuation, links starting with www. open directly with a notice when they cannot be opened, and links to workspace files open the file preview inside the app.
- In easy mode, step records and computer action cards in a reply appear in the order they happened, instead of steps always at the top and computer actions at the bottom.
- When you come back to a partner after a while, the last memory change and reply of the previous chat stay above the "last chat" divider instead of showing up under this visit's welcome.
- In a remote workspace, jumping from a file preview to its folder opens the right place instead of failing on a doubled path.
